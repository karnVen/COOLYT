## 🛡️ Translation & Purchasing Guidelines

- **For Japanese Literature (*Sōseki, Kawakami*):** Never buy unbranded or anonymously translated reprints. Stick with **Penguin Classics** (Meredith McKinney for *Kokoro*), **Picador** (Sam Bett & David Boyd for *Heaven*), or **NYRB Classics** (William F. Sibley for *The Gate*).
- **For Russian Classics (*Tolstoy, Bulgakov*):** 
  - For maximum budget value with classic English cadence: **Fingerprint! Publishing** (using Maude/Garnett translations).
  - For the truest Russian linguistic accuracy: **Penguin Classics** (Pevear & Volokhonsky).
- **Platforms:** On Amazon India and Flipkart, filter for **Prime / Fulfilled by Amazon** (*Cocoblu Retail*, *Trans Infopreneur*) or **f-Assured** on Flipkart to avoid bootleg duplicate copies.
